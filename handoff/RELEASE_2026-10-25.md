# Production release — 2026-10-25 — slot acquisition and AI timing

Production: https://thangldw.github.io/jetrise/

## Authorized scope

The user explicitly selected integration and production publication of both the experimental per-hour acquisition cap and cost-aware expanded AI timing, after reviewing the remaining LHR edit/revert loophole and frequency restrictions. This release does not claim that all idle-slot exploits are fixed.

Additional player acquisitions cannot raise an hour above one owned movement. Existing higher quantities and their prices/commitment dates remain valid while held. Voluntary reductions do not reserve repurchase above the cap. Initial market activation still buys the full rights required to preserve existing flights and committed legs. Schema1, SAVE_KEY, schedules and saved tickets are unchanged; no automatic migration, reclaim or career reset occurs.

Expanded per-aircraft AI compares affordable legal clocks by signing plus seven days of quoted upkeep, retaining the previous clock ordering for ties. Canonical quotes, forecast, reviews and actual settlement remain shared. Legacy AI without network expansion keeps its old timing rule.

The prior isolated matrix found that LHR/economy 2/high fares/edit-revert can retain twelve unused paid rights, gain $119,749 after 180 days and reduce AI by twelve legs relative to its same-engine control. The user accepted this bounded release. Existing all-hour ownership may still exclude AI. No new pricing, expiry or sharing terms are retroactively imposed.

Manual artifact-only publication uses existing authenticated Git access. No GitHub App, deploy credential, backend, Cloudflare infrastructure or billing changes are included. Source, tests, fixtures and reports remain in the private source repository; public Pages receives build artifacts only.
